## 📝 Problem 58

### Problem Statement
Find the maximum and minimum element in an array.

### Test Cases

#### Case 1
- **Input:**
  ```text
5
2 9 1 4 7
  ```
- **Output:** `Max=9, Min=1`

#### Case 2
- **Input:**
  ```text
3
10 10 10
  ```
- **Output:** `Max=10, Min=10`
